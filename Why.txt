A0 — Orchestrator / Planner
Planning
This agent acts like a project manager.

It looks at:
- Document name.
- Document type.
- Available evidence.
- Document characteristics.
- What analysis is required.
For example, it sees DPDP_Synthetic_DPA_Test.pdf and determines that it is a Data Processing Agreement.

It creates an execution plan for the other agents.

It does not produce the final compliance report.
A1 — Document Intelligence
Understanding the document
This agent understands what the document actually says.

For your sample agreement, it should identify:
- Parties involved.
- Data Fiduciary.
- Data Processor.
- Purpose of the agreement.
- Contractual responsibilities.
- Security clauses.
- Retention and deletion clauses.
- Missing information.
Example:

Original text:
The processor shall not appoint a sub-processor without prior written authorisation from the fiduciary.

AI interpretation:
The agreement requires the Data Processor to obtain written approval from the Data Fiduciary before engaging any additional third-party processor.

Notice what happened?

The AI didn't simply copy the sentence. It converted legal language into understandable business language without changing its meaning.

This is one of the important things your project is supposed to accomplish.

A3 — Processing Activities / RoPA
Understanding why data is processed
RoPA means Records of Processing Activities.

This is one of the most valuable outputs of your project.

It identifies what an organization does with personal data.

From your PDF, we already have four explicit processing activities:
Activity	Purpose	System
Account administration	Maintain accounts	Cloud database
Customer support	Resolve support requests	Support platform
Service monitoring	Detect errors	Monitoring service
Backups	Restore services	Backup storage

The AI should expand these into structured processing records.

For example:

Business process: Customer Support

Processing purpose: Receiving, routing and resolving customer support requests.

Personal data: Names, email addresses, support-message contents and order references.

Processor: Example Cloud Services Pvt. Ltd.

System: Example Support Platform.

Retention: Not precisely specified.

Verification: Pending human review.

This is what your project should ultimately generate in its RoPA register.

A5 — DPDP Compliance Mapping
Compliance assessment
This agent compares the extracted information with the DPDP requirements catalogue.

For example:

Your document states:
The processor shall retain personal data only for the duration needed to provide the service.

But it does not specify an exact number of days or months.

The agent should identify:
- What requirement is relevant.
- What evidence exists.
- What information is missing.
- Whether the finding is evidenced, partially evidenced or requires verification.
- What action is recommended.
Example:
Field	Result
Requirement	Retention and deletion
Evidence	Contract section 7
Status	Requires verification
Gap	No exact retention period
Recommendation	Define a retention schedule and deletion timeline

Important: a missing detail in the agreement does not automatically prove that the organization violates the law. It may simply mean the available document does not establish that the requirement is fulfilled.
A6 — Risk and Gap Analysis
Identifying problems
This agent takes the previous analysis and identifies risks.

Your sample agreement deliberately leaves four points unclear:
1. No precise retention period.
2. No actual hosting regions.
3. No detailed sub-processor register.
4. No measurable incident-notification deadline.
These are exactly the kinds of gaps the system should identify.

A possible recommendation:
Establish a documented retention schedule specifying the applicable retention period for each personal data category. Define deletion procedures and maintain deletion evidence.

It can also suggest:
- Priority.
- Responsible department.
- Suggested remediation timeline.
- Risk rating.
- Supporting evidence.
A7 — Quality Assurance
Quality control
This agent acts like a senior reviewer checking the other AI agents.

It should check:
- Are all required fields present?
- Do evidence IDs actually exist?
- Are the compliance mappings valid?
- Are findings supported by the source document?
- Are there contradictions?
- Are any conclusions invented?
- Are important gaps missing?
If it identifies problems, your workflow can request corrections from the relevant agent.

Your JSON contains a maximum of two automated QA revision cycles.

This is important because AI can make mistakes even when its initial answer looks convincing.
